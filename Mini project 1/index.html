PIZZA

🍕 Ingredients for Pizza

  1. For the pizza base
    • Pizza base (ready-made) — 1
    • OR pizza dough made with flour, yeast, water, salt, and oil
  2. For the pizza sauce
    • Pizza sauce — 2–3 tablespoons
    • OR tomato ketchup — 2 tablespoons
  3. For toppings
    • Onion — 1 small
    • Capsicum — 1 small
    • Tomato — 1 small
    • Sweet corn — 2 tablespoons
    • Olives or mushrooms — optional
  4. For cheese and seasoning
    • Mozzarella cheese — ½ to 1 cup, grated
    • Oregano — ½ teaspoon
    • Chilli flakes — ½ teaspoon
    • Salt — a little, if needed

BRIEF DESCRIPTION OF THE DISH

This is a Vegetable Pizza 🍕 It is a delicious pizza made with a crispy base, tangy tomato sauce , melted mozzarella cheese, and fresh vegetables like onion ,capsicum ,tomato , and sweet corn. It is topped with oregano and chilli flakes to add extra flavour. It is cheesy, colourful, and perfect for a tasty snack or meal.
